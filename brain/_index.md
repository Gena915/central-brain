## How to navigate this

Every folder has an `_index.md` that says **what it is** and **what's inside** (a one-line
hook per child). To move or save, use the skills:

- `/continue` → asks project → sub-area → section until the exact spot, and **loads all
  that info** into the chat to keep working.
- `/save` → after working, asks down to the right section and **saves complete info**
  (updates or creates). It's a stickler on purpose.

Organization rules: see [CONVENTIONS.md](./CONVENTIONS.md).
