# knowledge/ — the Brain's manual + guide map

> The Brain's **manual and map**: a concrete summary of the whole brain + the **routes** to
> where the detail of each thing lives. This is also where the distilled, reusable stuff
> (methods, learnings) lives — what the Brain consults BEFORE executing.

**Path:** knowledge/
**Last updated:** seeded on install

---

## Role: manual + guide map

`knowledge/` is the **navigable master index**: a summary of what's in each bucket and
**where to go** for the detail (routes to `work/`, `personal/`, `tools/`, `agents/`…). It's
what a fresh agent reads to orient quickly without opening everything.

## Sections

- [methods/](./methods/) — how to work: proven ways of working and methodologies.
- [learnings/](./learnings/) — lessons learned. What NOT to repeat and what DOES work.

## How the Brain uses it

For every task, the Brain sweeps `knowledge/` to (a) orient itself on the brain map and (b)
decide how to execute best, given proven methods and prior learnings. The catalog of
concrete tools lives in [`tools/`](../tools/).
